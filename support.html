Will it run on my PC?

Open Project Bunny and look at Settings → Local AI models → Your computer before downloading anything.

  • Voice diary, titles and feelings: any Windows 10 (2004 or later) or Windows 11 PC with 8 GB of memory. 16 GB is better.
  • Painted pictures: an NVIDIA graphics card with 6 GB or more of graphics memory.
  • Space: about 12 GB free for the AI models.

Common questions

The model download stopped. Open Settings and press the download again - it carries on from where it stopped.

A picture came out wrong (an extra hand, the wrong person...). Open the page and press "Something wrong with this picture?", or repaint it. You can also tap a person under the picture to say who they are.

My phone can't open the "From my phone" page. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi. If Windows asked whether Project Bunny may use the network, allow it on private networks. Guest or public Wi-Fi often blocks this.

How do I move my diary to a new PC? Settings → Your diary → Back up your diary. Copy the .bunny file to the new PC, install Project Bunny and choose Restore from a backup.

I forgot my PIN. Press Forgot your PIN? on the lock screen. You can confirm it's you with Windows Hello (your Windows PIN, fingerprint or face), type the recovery code you were shown when you set the PIN, or ask for a reset and choose a new PIN after 3 days. If someone else asks for a reset, you're told the next time you open your diary with your PIN, and their request is cancelled.

My trial ended. Your diary is still there to read, print and back up. Buy Project Bunny once in the Microsoft Store to write new pages - no subscription.
